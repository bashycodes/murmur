# NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5: better than our models, fit for the home server? (2026-10-28)

**Question (owner).** "Check its stats to understand if it's potentially better than our
current model … Make sure it's suitable for my CPU. If it's not … tell me what would be
needed in order to run it", because the backend may move to a machine with a GPU.

## TL;DR

* **What it is.** "Nemotron 3.5" is a *family*, not one model. Five members matter to us:
  | model | kind | released | size | role for us |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **Lightning 30B-A3B** | text LLM (reasoning on/off) | 2026-08-11 | 30B total, 3B active MoE | AI cleanup, **cloud** (OpenRouter) |
  | **Bolt E4B "4GB"** | small text LLM (Gemma-4-E4B post-train) | 2026-10-23 | 8B total, 4B effective | AI cleanup, **local** |
  | **Bolt 74B-A9B "24GB"** | LLM, 2-bit QAT | 2026-10-15 | 74B total, 9B active | local on a 24 GB GPU |
  | **Super VL 120B-A12B** | multimodal LLM | 2026-10-15 | 120B total, 12B active | cloud only |
  | **Transcribe 30B-A4B** | speech-to-text (ASR) | **2026-10-28 (today)** | 34B total, 4.3B active | ASR, GPU only |

  The project started on 2026-10-17, so the members that "came out after we started" are
  **Bolt E4B** (10-23) and **Transcribe** (10-28). Super VL / Bolt 74B launched two days
  before. The owner most likely means the LLM family (the launch news); Transcribe is
  covered because it competes with Parakeet. A sixth, `nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b`
  (2026-06), was already rejected in `docs/proposals/stt-model-survey.md` (weak English).
* **Better than our current models?**
  * **Cloud cleanup: no, cheaper but worse (measured 2026-10-29).** Lightning on OpenRouter
    costs **$0.07 / $0.20 per M tokens** vs **$0.10 / $0.32** for our Llama-3.3-70B and has
    ZDR endpoints. Prompt v4c, the same 102 cases (`results/or_v4c_nemo_lightning.jsonl` vs
    `or_v4c_l70_r2.jsonl` in `tools/probes/prototypes/ai_cleanup_bench_2026_10_26/`):

    | | Llama-3.3-70B | Lightning (reasoning off) |
    |---|---|---|
    | 88-suite pass | **90%** | 81% |
    | reference exact / closeness (14) | **4/14 / 0.910** | 1/14 / 0.860 |
    | guard rejects (good rejected) | 1 (0) | 5 (1) |
    | LLM ms p50 / p90 | 421 / 728 | **373 / 485** |
    | $ per call | 0.00159 | **0.00012** (13× cheaper) |

    Its failures: drops "Okay"/"Yeah" openers, misplaces close-quotes and dashes, keeps
    "First/Second" instead of a numbered list. Keep Llama-3.3-70B as the default; Lightning
    is a usable budget pick. **Reasoning must be off**: it reasons by default, and with
    reasoning on, every call hit `max_completion_tokens` with empty content
    (`or_v4c_nemo_lightning_reasoning_on.jsonl`, 106/106). The app sends `reasoning:
    {effort: "none"}` when the model's catalogue entry lists `reasoning`; a free-text model
    id looked up by `catalog::lookup_model` lost that list until 2026-10-29 (now fixed).
  * **Local cleanup on this CPU: no.** Bolt E4B is the only member that fits in RAM;
    estimated **~3.5–5 s per dictation** (between our measured 3B at 2.9 s and 8B at 7.5 s),
    2–3× the 1.5 s budget. Everything else does not fit in 19 GB RAM.
  * **ASR: more accurate on paper, not runnable here.** Transcribe has the best published
    open English WER (5.33 avg Open ASR) vs ≈6.0 for Parakeet v2 / unified, but it is a
    34B offline model, served on GPUs (H100 / L40S / RTX 5090), with no CPU runtime yet.
* **Fits the CPU (i7-6700, 4C/8T AVX2, no AVX-512, 19 GB RAM, no GPU)?** Only Bolt E4B
  loads, and it misses the latency budget. **All numbers for this CPU are estimated**: no
  official GGUF of any member is ≤ 3 GB, so nothing was downloaded or measured.
* **GPU needed.** Bolt E4B: any NVIDIA card with **≥ 8 GB VRAM** (uses 3.5–5 GB), e.g. an
  RTX 4060/5060 or RTX 3060 12 GB. Lightning, Bolt 74B, Transcribe: a **32 GB RTX 5090**
  (or a 24 GB card with a 3-bit quant / CPU offload) plus 32–64 GB system RAM. Super VL:
  data-centre (4× H100), use OpenRouter.

## The models

### Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B-A3B (text LLM)

* Hybrid Mamba-2 + MoE + some attention layers, with multi-token prediction; **30B total,
  3B active**; context up to 1M (256K on one H100); released **2026-08-11**; licence
  **OpenMDW-1.1**, commercial use OK
  ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16)).
  Artificial Analysis counts 31.6B / 3.6B active
  ([AA launch article](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/nemotron-3-5-lightning-launch)).
* Reasoning is **on by default**, switched off with `enable_thinking=False`; recommended
  sampling temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95 ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16)).
* Benchmarks (NVIDIA harness): MMLU-Pro **81.94**, GPQA Diamond 75.44, **IFBench (loose)
  71.88**; no IFEval ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16)).
  AA Intelligence Index **24** (= gpt-oss-120b; Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 32), ~670 tok/s on a
  DeepInfra NVFP4 endpoint ([AA](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/nemotron-3-5-lightning-launch)).
  None of these measure rewriting/formatting short text, which is our task.
* Languages: English primary; es, fr, de, it, ja; post-training also zh ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16)).
* **GGUF / llama.cpp:** ggml-org ships Q4_0 **18.9 GB**, Q8_0 33.6 GB, BF16 63.2 GB
  ([ggml-org GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-GGUF));
  community Q3_K_M 19.8 GB, Q4_K_M ≈ 25 GB ([runaihome guide](https://runaihome.com/blog/nemotron-35-lightning-consumer-gpu-hardware-guide-2026/)).
  Also NVFP4 and W4A16 for vLLM / SGLang / TensorRT-LLM ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-BF16)).
* **OpenRouter** (`nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning`, public `/api/v1/models` and
  `/endpoints`, read 2026-10-28): **$0.07 in / $0.20 out per M** (list), 262K context,
  `reasoning.mandatory = false`, providers Darkbloom int4 ($0.025/$0.12), Io Net, DeepInfra
  bf16 ($0.06/$0.16), CoreWeave bf16; **ZDR endpoints: DeepInfra, CoreWeave, Io Net**
  (`/api/v1/endpoints/zdr`) ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning)).
  A `:free` variant also exists; it was not checked against our ZDR / no-data-collection settings.
  For comparison Llama-3.3-70B: $0.10 / $0.32 ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct)).

### Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B "4GB" (small LLM, the local candidate)

* A post-train of **Gemma-4-E4B-it**; **8B total, 4B effective**; 64K context; released
  **2026-10-23**; OpenMDW-1.1 (base Gemma 4 Apache-2.0); English, es, fr, de, it, ja (+ zh in
  post-training); reasoning on by default, `enable_thinking=False` or llama-server
  `--reasoning-budget` to limit it
  ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB)).
* "4GB" is the **VRAM** target, not the file: the official Q4_K_M GGUF is **6.18 GB**
  ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB)). Community
  quants go down to Q2_K **4.4 GB** (Q4_K_M 5.34 GB)
  ([prithivMLmods GGUF](https://huggingface.co/prithivMLmods/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-GGUF));
  ggml-org has Q8_0 8.03 GB / BF16 15.1 GB plus a 596 MB DFlash *draft* model, which is
  not a standalone model ([ggml-org GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-GGUF)).
  Runs on llama.cpp (gguf arch `gemma4`), Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, SGLang ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB)).
* Instruction following (Q4_K_M, reasoning on): **IFBench 64.00** vs Gemma-4-E4B-it 42.67,
  Qwen3.5-4B 66.67; InverseIFEval 45.26 vs 39.75 / 22.04 ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-4GB)).
  NVIDIA's BF16 IHEval 84.8 vs Qwen3.5-4B 60.6 ([summary of NVIDIA's table](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/26810/nemotron-3-5-bolt-e4b-nvidia-robots-games)).
  No IFEval/MMLU.
* Speed (NVIDIA, llama.cpp CUDA, batch 1, 3 000-token prompt, 50 output tokens): RTX 5070
  **133.4 tok/s, TTFT 548 ms**; RTX Spark 67.0 / 930 ms; Jetson Orin Nano 8 GB 15.5 /
  5 785 ms. VRAM 3.5 GB (3K prompt) to 5.1 GB (64K). **No CPU numbers published**
  ([summary of NVIDIA's table](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/26810/nemotron-3-5-bolt-e4b-nvidia-robots-games)).
* Not on OpenRouter (absent from the `/api/v1/models` list, 2026-10-28).

### Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B-A9B "24GB"

* Mamba-2–Transformer LatentMoE + vision encoder, **74B total / 9B active**, 2-bit
  quantisation-aware training (experts IQ2-XXS, Mamba NVFP4) to fit **24 GB**; 256K
  context; 2026-10-15; OpenMDW-1.1; vLLM (nightly) on A100/H100/Blackwell, tested on RTX
  5090, DGX Spark, RTX Spark; no CPU support stated
  ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB)).
  NVIDIA lists llama.cpp/Ollama/LM Studio support ([NVIDIA blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/nemotron-3-5-super-vl/)).
* Agentic/reasoning scores above Lightning (GPQA 86.43 vs 75.57); no IFBench reported
  ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-74B-A9B-24GB)). Not on OpenRouter.

### Nemotron 3.5 Super VL 120B-A12B

* **120B total / 12B active** hybrid MoE, text + image + video, up to 1M context,
  2026-10-15, OpenMDW-1.1; IFBench **70.86**; deployment 4× H100 (TP4) or 2× GB200
  ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Super-VL-120B-A12B-BF16)).
* OpenRouter `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl`: **$0.15 / $0.50 per M**, 1M context, reasoning
  optional, 8 ZDR endpoints (DeepInfra, Together, CoreWeave, …)
  ([model page](https://openrouter.ai/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl)). Dearer than our 70B
  on output and only needed if Lightning falls short.

### Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe 30B-A4B (speech-to-text)

* Speech encoder (~0.8B) + Mamba-Transformer MoE LLM; **34B total, 4.3B active**; 40
  languages; up to 1 h recordings; released **2026-10-28**; OpenMDW-1.1
  ([model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B)).
* **WER:** Open ASR Leaderboard 9-set mean **4.71** (Qwen3-ASR-1.7B 4.94); long-form
  **10.98** vs Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v3 13.37 ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B));
  leaderboard average **5.33 %**, #1 open model; AA-WER 2.8 %
  ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-transcribe-granary)). Our survey
  lists Parakeet v2 at 6.02 / 6.05 and unified ≈ 5.91 on the same leaderboard
  (`docs/proposals/stt-model-survey.md`).
* Promptable: verbatim or cleaned transcript, punctuation/capitalisation, written-form
  numbers, vocabulary hints, speaker tags, translation ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B)).
  This overlaps with our AI cleanup step.
* **Offline only** ("This is an offline model"), so no live preview / D22 streaming
  ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B)).
* Hardware: recommended 1× H100 80 GB; A100 80 GB BF16, L40S 48 GB FP8, **RTX 5090 NVFP4**,
  RTX PRO 6000; runtimes NeMo, vLLM 0.32 + NeMo plugin, Transformers, SGLang-Omni, GGML
  ([card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Transcribe-30B-A4B)). The quantised
  builds and NeMo-Speech.cpp local runtime are "coming soon"; no CPU figures
  ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-transcribe-granary)).
  Not on OpenRouter.

## This machine

`lscpu` / `nproc` / `free` on the home server (2026-10-28): Intel Core i7-6700 @ 3.4 GHz
(Skylake), 4 cores / 8 threads, AVX2 + FMA, no AVX-512, 8 MB L3; **19.4 GB RAM, ~4.9 GB
available** with murmur-server and other jobs running; no GPU. `docs/model-benchmarks.md`
puts its memory bandwidth at ≈ 34 GB/s (dual-channel DDR4-2133). The repo has no specs for
the owner's Windows PC, so it is not assessed here.

## CPU fit (estimated, not measured)

Why not measured: the brief allowed a download of a ≤ 3 GB GGUF; the smallest Nemotron 3.5
LLM file is Bolt E4B Q2_K at 4.4 GB, and only 4.9 GB RAM was free.

| model | smallest GGUF | fits 19 GB RAM next to murmur-server? | est. per dictation, 4 threads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bolt E4B | 4.4 GB (Q2_K), 5.3–6.2 GB (Q4_K_M) | barely: ~5–6 GB RSS vs ~4.9 GB free now; murmur-server would have to drop its Qwen 1.5B | **~3.5–5 s** (budget 1.5 s) |
| Lightning 30B-A3B | 18.9 GB (Q4_0) | **no** (total RAM 19.4 GB) | n/a (decode would be ~3B-class if it fit) |
| Bolt 74B-A9B | ~24 GB | **no** | n/a |
| Super VL 120B | > 60 GB | **no** | n/a |
| Transcribe 34B | no CPU build yet; BF16 ≈ 68 GB | **no** | n/a |

How the Bolt E4B estimate is built: on this CPU decode is memory-bandwidth bound and
prefill compute bound (`docs/model-benchmarks.md`). Bolt E4B computes like a 4B dense model
(the extra 4B of Gemma "E" models are per-layer embeddings, looked up, not multiplied).
Measured here: Qwen2.5-3B (1.96 GB) pp 23 / tg 6.5–10 tok/s and **2.9 s p50** per
dictation; Llama-3.1-8B (4.9 GB) **7.5 s p50** (`2026-10-26-ai-cleanup-benchmark.md`).
4B-effective at Q4 sits between them: ≈ 15–20 tok/s prefill, ≈ 5–8 tok/s decode, so
~3.5–5 s p50 with thinking **off**. With thinking on (the default) it would generate
hundreds of hidden tokens first: tens of seconds. A current desktop CPU (DDR5, ~2–3× the
bandwidth) might halve it, still over budget. **Not CPU-suitable for the latency budget.**

**Concrete example machine.** A Linux desktop with **one RTX 5090 (32 GB)**, a current
8–16-core CPU and **64 GB RAM** runs any single one of Lightning Q4, Bolt 74B, or
Transcribe NVFP4 (when it ships), plus Parakeet on CUDA. If the goal is only fast local AI
cleanup, a **12 GB RTX 3060/5070 with 32 GB RAM** is enough for Bolt E4B (or Qwen2.5-3B/7B,
per `docs/model-benchmarks.md` § Desktop GPU).

## Verdict

1. **Cloud AI cleanup: worth a benchmark run now.** Lightning is the only Nemotron 3.5
   model that could beat the current production choice on cost (≈ 30–40 % cheaper than
   Llama-3.3-70B per token) at likely similar latency (3B active; ~600–670 tok/s served).
   Whether it is *better* on our task is unknown: it is a reasoning-first 3B-active model,
   and published scores (IFBench, MMLU-Pro) do not measure formatting. Run the existing
   suite through the product cleaner:
   `PROMPT_VERSION=v4c SPENT_GLOB='or_v4c*' CAP=0.50 tools/probes/prototypes/ai_cleanup_bench_2026_10_26/run_or.sh or_v4c_nemo_lightning nvidia/nemotron-3.5-lightning`
   (102 calls × ~2 K prompt tokens ≈ **$0.02**). `crates/murmur-cloud/src/cleaner.rs` already
   sends `reasoning.effort = "none"` for models where reasoning is optional, which OpenRouter
   reports for Lightning. Accept if ≥ 90 % on the 88-suite, reference closeness ≥ 0.91, p50 ≤
   0.75 s. Super VL ($0.15/$0.50) only if Lightning falls short.
2. **Local AI cleanup on this i7-6700: no.** Bolt E4B is too slow (~3.5–5 s, estimated)
   and squeezes RAM; the bigger members do not fit. Keep Qwen2.5-1.5B locally.
3. **On a future GPU box:** benchmark Bolt E4B (thinking off) against Qwen2.5-3B/7B with
   `tools/probes/prototypes/ai_cleanup_bench_2026_10_26/run.sh` (it takes any GGUF;
   murmur-llm needs llama.cpp support for `gemma4`). NVIDIA's instruction-following scores
   put it well above Gemma-4-E4B and level with Qwen3.5-4B.
4. **ASR: not now.** Transcribe is more accurate on paper than Parakeet but is offline-only,
   GPU-only, and its quantised/local builds are not out yet. Revisit when NeMo-Speech.cpp
   or an NVFP4 build ships, and only on an RTX 5090-class box. Its promptable "clean
   transcript" mode could replace part of AI cleanup there.

## Uncertain

* All CPU and consumer-GPU per-dictation times are estimates; nothing was run.
* Some numbers come from secondary write-ups of NVIDIA's tables (Bolt E4B speed/VRAM,
  Lightning consumer-GPU speeds); the model cards did not include them.
* Parameter counts differ by source (Lightning 30B vs 31.6B; Transcribe 30B vs 34B).
* OpenRouter prices and ZDR endpoints are a 2026-10-28 snapshot; free (`:free`) routes
  were not considered.
